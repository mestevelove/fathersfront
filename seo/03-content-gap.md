# Content Gap

Use connected DataForSEO tools to compare `fathersfront.com` with the most
relevant organic competitors found in the current search results. Select
competitors by shared search visibility, not by superficial brand similarity.

Return:

1. Competitor domains selected and why each is relevant.
2. Valuable keywords and topic clusters competitors rank for that Fathers
   Front does not.
3. Gaps already covered conceptually by existing Fathers Front pages but not
   discoverable because of weak targeting, titles, internal links, or depth.
4. Gaps that genuinely justify new content.
5. A prioritized content plan with target intent, proposed URL, supporting
   pages, internal-link sources, and conversion path.

Exclude irrelevant legal-directory, law-firm, government, and mass-publisher
advantages unless Fathers Front can realistically compete with a distinct
education, lived-experience, book, community, or advisory angle.
