# Keyword Research

Build a keyword strategy for `fathersfront.com` using connected DataForSEO
data. Focus on fathers facing divorce, custody conflict, parental alienation,
international child abduction, family-court pressure, and the rebuilding of
health, income, leadership, and family relationships.

Return:

1. Keyword clusters grouped by intent: informational, problem-aware,
   solution-aware, book/product, coaching/advisory, community, and speaking.
2. For each cluster, include representative keywords, volume, competition,
   intent, and the strongest current or proposed destination URL.
3. Identify sensitive or legally risky phrasing that should be handled as
   education rather than promises, representation, or legal advice.
4. Separate opportunities supported by data from brand-language ideas that
   have little or no measurable demand.
5. Produce a build order: optimize an existing page, create a new article, or
   defer.

Do not recommend a new page when an existing page can satisfy the intent.
